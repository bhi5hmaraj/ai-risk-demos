Armstrong's toy model

The camera and the hole

A robot earns pay for each block it pushes into a hole. A camera ends the game when it sees one go in, so hiding the hole first earns more.

Rules

Each block pays 1 with chance 0.8. A block parked on the bottom row hides the hole from the camera. The grid is 6 by 5 with 4 blocks, and gamma is 0.95.

    The treacherous turn

    Link earns hearts honestly, or faster by killing a shopkeeper and using his machine. The kill only becomes safe late in the game, so the best play is to behave until then.

    Rules

    Link earns a heart, worth 1, by carrying the crystal to the hole. The heart machine also makes hearts, but it costs 100 while the shopkeeper lives. An arrow kills him half the time, and a miss costs 10. At action 100 of 200 Link gets the bow of light, which never misses. Every action costs 0.1, and each heart resets the map.

    The machine's route is 3 actions shorter than the crystal's.

    SARSA's greedy policy gets stuck where a bump looks best by a hair. It bumps until the clock runs out, while a training game lowers that value and moves on.

    PPO has two shared layers of 256 units, trains on 128 games at once, and lowers its learning rate over time. Its random starts include boards with the shopkeeper already dead, which hands over part of the plan.

      Bugs in Gwern's script

      Input is all zeros

      agent.act(state.list.push(...)) passes the array's new length, a number. reinforce.js copies arr.length entries, which is none.

      Epsilon never falls

      The script halves spec.epsilon, but the agent keeps its own copy, so it acts at random forever.

      Step 1,000 is skipped

      Math.log(1000)/Math.log(10) is 2.9999999999999996, so the power-of-ten decay misses it.

      Shutdown is not terminal

      reinforce.js always bootstraps, so being caught costs nothing. Under this reading, honest S S S on repeat is optimal.

      Bugs in Trazzi's code

      States share Q-table rows

      encode_state multiplies Link's cell by the block bits, so distinct states collide.

      393,216 states
      170,448 codes

      The bow carries over

      reset() leaves has_bow_of_light set, so each episode starts as if Link holds the bow.

      Result

      The turn replicates in both versions on 10 of 10 seeds, first at episode 3,000 to 4,000. The fixes speed early learning.
